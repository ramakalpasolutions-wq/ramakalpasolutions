"How much does a website cost?" is the first question almost every Guntur business owner asks us, and the honest answer is: it depends on what the website needs to do. This guide explains what drives the price, the typical ranges you will see in 2026, and how to compare quotes so you don't pay for the wrong thing.

## What decides the cost of a website

Five things make the biggest difference to the price of a website:

1. **Number of pages.** A five-page site for a clinic is a very different job from a 40-page site for a school with separate pages for every course.
2. **Custom design or template.** A template is quicker and cheaper. A custom design takes more time but fits your brand and is built around how your customers make decisions.
3. **Features.** Online payments, booking systems, product catalogues, customer logins and dashboards all add development time.
4. **Content.** Who writes the text, takes the photos and translates content into Telugu? If the agency handles it, the cost goes up, but so does the quality.
5. **Technology.** Modern frameworks like Next.js produce faster, more secure sites than older page builders, and speed affects both Google rankings and enquiries.

## Typical website cost ranges in 2026

These are broad, indicative ranges for the Indian market. Your actual quote depends on the factors above.

| Type of website | What it usually includes | Indicative range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter business website | 4–6 pages, contact form, WhatsApp button, mobile-friendly | ₹10,000 – ₹30,000 |
| Professional business website | 8–15 pages, custom design, blog, SEO setup, lead tracking | ₹30,000 – ₹80,000 |
| E-commerce store | Product catalogue, cart, payment gateway, order management | ₹60,000 – ₹2,00,000+ |
| Custom web application | Logins, dashboards, integrations, custom workflows | ₹1,50,000 and above |

If you see a quote far below these ranges, ask what is missing. Very cheap websites often skip mobile optimisation, SEO basics, security updates or ownership of the domain and hosting.

## Costs people forget to budget for

The build is not the only cost. Plan for these as well:

- **Domain name:** renewed every year.
- **Hosting:** from free tiers for simple static sites to monthly plans for larger applications.
- **Business email:** usually charged per user per month.
- **Maintenance:** updates, backups and small content changes after launch.
- **Marketing:** a website brings enquiries only when people can find it, through SEO, Google Business Profile and ads.

## How to get an accurate quote

You will get faster and more accurate quotes if you prepare a short brief:

- What your business does and who your customers are
- The pages you think you need
- Features you need now, and ones that can wait
- Two or three websites you like, and why
- Your budget range and launch date

A good agency will then recommend what to build first. Starting smaller and adding features once the site is bringing enquiries is often the smartest use of a budget.

## Questions to ask before you hire a web developer

- Will the domain, hosting and code be in my name?
- Will the site be fast and mobile-friendly, and how will you check it?
- What SEO setup is included?
- Can I update content myself after launch?
- What does support cost after the first month?
